# PACKWORKS card-set audit

The full 240-card rules pool was reviewed against two separate questions:

- **Impact:** Can this card anchor a display, multiply a mature engine, remove
  variance, accelerate opening, or cross a progression boundary?
- **Interest:** Does this card change sequencing, positioning, targeting, pack
  composition, resource conversion, or another rule of play?

Pure cash increases and large late-game percentages were not automatically
classified as interesting. A high-rarity card still had to create a meaningful
decision or engine interaction.

## Findings

- **Echo and Mark are the strongest general-purpose cores.** Echuneko, Resonash,
  Zeraph, Echoboard, and Omniecho turn frequent reveal events into reliable
  multipliers.
- **Fusion and Salvage create the largest chain reactions.** Heartmerge,
  Twinforge, Heartforge, Salvitan, Recyclora, and Peacescrap can turn one pack or
  sale into several new reveal events.
- **Position cards create the best display-case decisions.** Portalink,
  Questcap, Truescope, and Bluprintix reward exact slot order instead of simply
  asking the player to equip six large numbers.
- **The most distinctive pack rules are Locklure, Echowl, Pacturion, and
  Firstseer.** They respectively rewrite pack rarity, grow packs during Echo,
  reach locked sets, and reveal a whole pack at once.
- **Several late cards are powerful but intentionally not “interesting.”**
  Matchacho, Nightecho, Sparksigil, Chromaglow, and Solarcatalyst are excellent
  variance-removal tools; their value is consistency rather than a new rule.

The curated result contains 40 cards: 34 Impact picks, 25 Interesting picks,
with overlap between the two. The review gallery reads the shared audit in
`lib/cardAudit.js`, so filter badges and audit membership cannot drift apart.
